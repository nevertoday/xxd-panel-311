## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one hand-drawn pictogram, not a scene. Keep the one shape that names the subject, with a slightly clumsy designed proportion and an uneven hand edge. Material is colored pencil, wax pastel and textured paper. It should feel kept in an old book, a local sign or a private seal. Type is a tiny collection word. Reject a full building, a full figure, a full scene, smooth vector and machine-perfect edges.
